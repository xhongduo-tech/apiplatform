# Community Code of Conduct

We want participation in this project to be safe, constructive and welcoming.
Contributors must communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people,
protect private information, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats,
sexualized conduct or deliberate disruption.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when conduct harms the community or project. Reports should be sent privately
to the maintainers through the repository host. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.

This policy applies in project repositories, review discussions, community
channels and public events where a person represents the project.
